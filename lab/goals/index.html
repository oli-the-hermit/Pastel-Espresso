The goal card in 1.4 (after A7), with the app's own styles.

What I see
  1. Landmark rows show three loose tools on hover; tasks, lists and sessions now have one menu each. Landmarks can't be reordered.
  2. On a goal with landmarks, "Complete" looks like a button but does nothing: it completes on its own.
  3. "Landmarks" is said three times: the count, the overline, the button.
  4. "+ Landmark" sits at the bottom, away from the landmarks, and always opens a dialog, even for just a name.
  5. Every card shows "Write a description", written or not.
  6. The rows start 8px in, not on the lists' 16px grid; the card's sides are 12px plus 12px.

B1 · Landmarks as a list

The landmarks are a list in the card, with A7's heading: name, count, thin bar.

How it works
  • Band: the period as the overline, the menu, the title, then the reward and the dates.
  • Landmarks: a heading with the count and a thin bar, then raised rows with one quiet menu (Mark complete, Edit, Duplicate, Move up, Move down, Delete).
  • Adding: "Add a landmark" is the list's last row: type a name, press Enter. Dates and a reward come from Edit.
  • Completing: a goal with landmarks completes when the last one is ticked (a "Done" chip on the band). Only a goal without landmarks has a "Complete goal" button.
  • Description: shown once written; "Add a description" is in the menu.

B2 · Progress in the title

The goal is the list: its band carries the count and the bar, like a list's heading.

How it differs from B1
  • The count ("1 of 3") sits beside the menu on the overline's line, and the bar closes the band. No "Landmarks" heading: the rows follow the band directly.
  • Every card shows its progress in the same place, so a page of goals scans in one line per card.
  • Rows, adding, completing and the description work as in B1.
Chosen: B2 (2026-11-11)
  • A goal is a list of landmarks, so its band is the list's heading: the same name, count and bar as A7, one level up, without repeating the word.
  • Progress reads first, in the same spot on every card; that's what you look for on a page of goals.
  • The cost: the band is taller on goals with landmarks, and the bar sits a little away from the rows it counts.
