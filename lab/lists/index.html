What testing showed
  1. The list's name is a field: the same pill, fill and overline as the things inside it.
  2. Three chevrons with three meanings: pick a list, fold the list, add a list.
  3. The grip is darker than its row, so it reads as a gap, and row text starts further right than the header's.
  4. The check box's outline is the strongest mark in the row.
  5. "Add another task list" reads as a link, and its chevron as a dropdown.
  6. Session rows are raised and task rows recessed; session tools are loose icons, task tools a menu.
  7. The picker boxes its lists in a sunken panel, puts one icon in a tile on every row, and its "New task list" is a lime row unlike any other button.

The rules
  • Title block: every card starts with a band one surface step up. On it: the overline in the accent, the card's menu on the same line, the title 8px under. A long title is cut with an ellipsis at the band's padding.
  • Surfaces: rows are raised one step above what they sit on, one more when pointed at. What is empty and waiting for you is recessed: the check box, the "Add a task" field, the picker's search.
  • Check box: a dark hole with a soft hairline rim. Ticked: the accent.
  • One grid: chevron, check boxes and plus share a column; names and text share the next.
  • Grip: in the card's margin, on hover or keyboard focus.
  • Menus: one three-dot menu per thing, at its right end: the card, the list, the row.
  • Adding: at the end of what you add to, with the same neutral pill: a task, a list, a session, a new list in the picker.
  • Selection: the soft tint.
New in this round
  • Sessions card: a title (the selected session's name) and its own menu, like the task card.
  • Picker: round 4's A5 header with the search under it, A6's raised rows, the list icon back without its tile, and rows that scroll out of view on a rounded corner.
  • Picker's scroll bar: inside the padding, as far from the right edge as the rows are from the left. Rows make room for it only when there is one.
  • Search: a clear button in the accent once something is typed. Escape clears first, then closes.
  • Overline: in the accent, on the band.
  • The fourth session has a long name, to show how a row and the card's title cut it. Select it to see the title.
