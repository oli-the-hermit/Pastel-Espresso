What testing showed
  1. The list's name is a field: the same pill, fill and overline as the things inside it.
  2. Three chevrons with three meanings: pick a list, fold the list, add a list.
  3. The grip is darker than its row, so it reads as a gap, and row text starts further right than the header's.
  4. The check box's outline is the strongest mark in the row.
  5. "Add another task list" reads as a link, and its chevron as a dropdown.
  6. Session rows are raised and task rows recessed; session tools are loose icons, task tools a menu.
  7. The picker boxes its lists in a sunken panel, repeats one icon on every row, and its "New task list" is a lime row unlike any other button.
